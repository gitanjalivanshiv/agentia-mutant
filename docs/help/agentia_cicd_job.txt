Manage Copado job executions

USAGE
  $ agentia cicd job COMMAND

TOPICS
  cicd job log          Inspect Copado job execution logs
  cicd job result-file  Inspect Files linked to Copado job Results

COMMANDS
  cicd job get     Get a Copado job execution with ordered steps
  cicd job kill    Cancel a Copado job execution
  cicd job list    List Copado job executions
  cicd job open    Open a Copado job execution in the browser
  cicd job pause   Pause a Copado job execution (resumable cancel alias)
  cicd job resume  Resume outstanding steps for a Copado job execution
  cicd job run     Run all steps for a Copado job execution
