Get the schedule for a Copado Robotic Testing job/test

USAGE
  $ agentia testing job schedule COMMAND

COMMANDS
  testing job schedule get     Get the schedule for a Copado Robotic Testing
                               job/test
  testing job schedule remove  Remove the schedule from a Copado Robotic Testing
                               job/test
  testing job schedule set     Create or update a Copado Robotic Testing
                               job/test schedule
