Invite a member to a Copado AI workspace

USAGE
  $ agentia ai workspace member invite WORKSPACE --permission Access|Manage --user-id
    <value> [--json] [--timeout <value>]

ARGUMENTS
  WORKSPACE  Copado AI workspace UUID.

FLAGS
  --permission=<option>  (required) Workspace permission for the invited member.
                         <options: Access|Manage>
  --timeout=<value>      AI gateway timeout in seconds.
  --user-id=<value>      (required) Copado AI user ID to invite.

GLOBAL FLAGS
  --json  Format output as json.

DESCRIPTION
  Invite a member to a Copado AI workspace

EXAMPLES
  $ agentia ai workspace member invite 42d0b908-37b7-4565-9ec9-86eda40c9782 --user-id 12345 --permission Access

  $ agentia ai workspace member invite 42d0b908-37b7-4565-9ec9-86eda40c9782 --user-id 12345 --permission Manage --json
